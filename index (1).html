Questions

Straight answers.

I already have Instagram. Do I need a website?

Instagram shows what you do. A website makes you look established when people Google you, shows up on Google Maps, and stays yours even when an app changes its rules.

Will I own my domain and website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you keep everything if you ever leave.

How can you charge less than an agency?

We skip the long meetings and slow back-and-forth, and every client goes through the same tight process. Every page and post is checked before it goes live, and nothing is published without your approval.

Do I need to attend meetings?

No. You answer 10 questions on WhatsApp, send a few photos, and approve your posts in one message each month.

Is there still a government grant for this?

The PSG grant stopped taking new applications on 29 September 2026. That is why our prices are set to make sense without one.

What happens after the 6-month minimum?

Your plan continues month to month. Cancel any time with 30 days' notice.
